Time

The model steps 1 ms at a time; if your machine can't keep up, it simply runs slower.

Strings

Each family is an instrument. V1 families have one string per 30° of preferred orientation; the LGN families one per strip of visual field, left to right. Hover a family for what it does.

Output

MIDI: one channel per family (1–9).

What V1 sees

The mouse's screen, 120° × 80°. The thalamus (LGN) samples it with ON and OFF cells, and V1 neurons pool those into oriented receptive fields. Each dot and tick sits where that cell looks and lights up when it fires.

Inside the column

Every simulated neuron at its position in the model column: a cylinder of V1 400 µm across, pia at the top. Balls flash when they spike; sticks are each neuron's strongest inputs, lit when they carry a spike. Drag to turn, pinch or scroll to zoom, hover a ball to name it.
